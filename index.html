Marbel

The childhood cave game — throw, flick, enter the cave, win the power, knock your rival away.

Drag back from your marble and release to shoot. Scroll to zoom, drag elsewhere to look around, T for top view.

Paused

How to play

  1. Both players lob their marble from the line at the cave. Closest goes first.
  2. Without the power you can only flick weakly. Get your marble into the cave to win the power.
  3. With the power you flick hard: knock your rival away from the cave.
  4. Any hit scores points by how hard it was (1–4). A hit on a powered rival strips their power and takes 1 point. A hit also earns you another shot.
  5. A marble resting inside the cave is safe: touching it scores nothing unless you knock it out of the cave.
  6. A hit can — very rarely — shatter a marble. Its owner throws a new one from the line, without the power.
